Orthoptic Exercises

Page under construction!

     Orthopedic exercises are the kind of exercises that can be done without the supervision of the specialist, but on his recommendation.
       Pay attention to children who require monocular occlusion, it is not done for hours on end, but only in the short term and with eye exercises, we do not try to decrease the visual acuity in the healthy eye but to increase VA in the weak eye !
       If the child requires occlusion in both eyes, this will be done according to the visual acuity of each eye, the occlusion and exercise times being directly related to this.
